Delete your Seekers League account

This page is for the Seekers League website (seekers-league.com) and the Seekers League Android app, which is the same service. Both are run by Pierfrancesco Di Giuseppe. You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time. You do not need the app to do it: the website works the same.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open seekers-league.com (or the app) and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the round button with your initial, at the top right, to open your profile.
  3. Tap Account, then the Sign-in tab.
  4. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on that device.

If you created a community that still has active players, you are asked to hand it over to one of them or archive it first (in the community, under Manage), so its players keep their community.

What is erased

  • Your login and email address.
  • Your profile details: display name, Discord name, Herald rank, malediction.gg profile link and map pin.
  • Your card collection, your private decks and the games you logged by hand.
  • The feedback you sent.
  • Your notification devices and settings.
  • A match you are still playing is cancelled.

What is kept, and why

  • Your finished matches and tournament games stay in your opponents' history and in their communities under the name Deleted player, so other players' records and standings do not change. They are no longer tied to your name or email address.
  • Public decks and community notices you posted stay, under the name Deleted player.
  • A community you created is deleted if nobody else ever used it; otherwise it is archived.
  • Tournaments run from a Malediction Play Network event keep your public Play Network nickname, unlinked from your deleted account. Those details come from the public event page and are deleted with the tournament; an organizer can also remove them sooner.
  • Private backup copies of the database are deleted after at most 90 days.

The full details are in the privacy notice.

Can't sign in any more?

Ask us to delete the account: write to seekersleague (at) gmail (dot) com from the email address of the account (for a Google or Discord sign-in, the email address of that login), so we know the request is yours, or use Send a suggestion or help squash a bug at the bottom of the home page. Tell us the account's display name. We delete it within one month, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

Delete only some data

You can do most of it yourself without deleting the account: clear any profile field under Account, delete decks and games logged by hand, turn notifications off under Account › Notifications, and switch off anonymous statistics in the privacy notice. For anything else, write to us as above.
